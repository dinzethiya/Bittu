Find my size

Measure once,
shop with confidence.

Grab a soft measuring tape (or a piece of string and a ruler). Pick who you're shopping for below — it takes about a minute.

Wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage, just under the bust, for the band number. Then measure loosely around the fullest part of your bust. We'll do the maths.

Stand naturally and measure around the widest part of your hips, tape parallel to the floor.

Measure around your natural waistline, where you'd normally wear a trouser waistband. Keep the tape snug, not tight.

Our kidswear runs by age band — this gives a solid starting point, sized generously for room to grow.
